Install and get settled

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and download the app.
  2. Open the APK. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install. Installing this update keeps your existing Roam data.
  3. Open Roam and complete your introduction, including Home and the kinds of experiences you enjoy.
  4. In Settings → Nudge settings, allow notifications and enable the departure companion. Choose precise location when Android asks so Roam can recognize when you leave a place.
  5. Connect Google Calendar to read calendars already synced on your phone. Roam does not add or change your calendar events.

Use it your way

Nearby shows a few ideas around your location. Trips and Travel calendar keep future destinations and times together; open their ideas when you want to browse. Use a confirmed destination and its timezone for trips outside Colorado.

Your daily limit, quiet hours, Home rhythm, advance-trip reminders and suggestion feedback live in Settings. You can pause tracking in the persistent phone notification.

Open Roam after your phone restarts to resume enabled departure tracking. Android battery saving can delay reminders; the app’s phone settings links let you check that. Force-stopping Roam stops background work until you reopen it.

Current coverage

Live places come from OpenStreetMap. The app shows when a source is unavailable or a smaller search is being used. Photos are found automatically on venue pages or from mapped Wikimedia files; some places have no usable photos.

Live event refresh currently covers the official Red Rocks calendar. Google Maps destinations launched or shared with Roam are remembered. Google Maps does not let Roam automatically read every active route in that other app.
